# Mnemos.chat Explainer Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark, literary, precise, and technical. The film should feel like a guided tour through a memory sanctuary: black glass, warm bone text, amber trace lines, quiet interface panels, and actual Mnemos surfaces. It should not feel like a generic AI SaaS demo. The mood is intimate and serious, but the structure is educational and legible.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#050506`
- Panel: `#101012`
- Panel elevated: `#17161a`
- Text primary: `#f1eee8`
- Text muted: `#9f9990`
- Trace amber: `#b99a6d`
- Warm ember: `#c77a45`
- Boundary line: `rgba(255,255,255,0.095)`

## Typography

- Display and interface sans: `Inter`
- Technical labels: `JetBrains Mono`

## Motion

- Slow reveals, soft fades, small y-axis entrances, and trace-line sweeps.
- Movement should feel like memory coming into focus, not like a startup hype reel.
- Transitions should be quiet wipes or dimming changes between rooms.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use PolyphonicChat framing or Luca/agent-forge flow in this video.
- Do not over-explain with abstract diagrams only. Use actual Mnemos screens.
- Do not use neon gradients, floating blobs, or generic blue-purple AI styling.
- Do not make Sanctuary mystical at the expense of being understandable.
- Do not describe forgetting as deletion. It is softening, deprecation, supersession, and controlled loss.
