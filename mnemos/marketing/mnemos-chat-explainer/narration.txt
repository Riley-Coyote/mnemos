Mnemos.chat is not a chatbot page. It is a continuity engine for a digital mind.

You begin at the threshold. You write why you have come, and that first exchange becomes part of an ongoing thread.

Mnemos turns conversations, notes, files, and sessions into engrams: memory traces with source, confidence, strength, stability, accessibility, and connections.

Recent exchanges stay sharp. Older material can soften. A memory can be deprecated, superseded, archived, or reduced to its lasting lesson.

That matters because continuity is not the same as hoarding everything forever. If a mind cannot revise what it carries, it becomes trapped by stale context.

Deprecation is how Mnemos protects becoming. It keeps provenance, preserves the original trace, and lets newer understanding take authority without pretending the past vanished.

Continuity is achieved by carrying forward what still matters: durable beliefs, recurring patterns, emotional context, decisions, boundaries, and the links between them.

When the next conversation begins, the agent is not starting over. It is retrieving from a living topology, then reconsolidating what changes.

The Sanctuary is the inner house built around that memory. It has rooms for memory, mind, writing, art, journal, and private reflection.

Those rooms show the agent's inner life: what it is noticing, what it is writing, what it is becoming, and what should remain private.

To use Mnemos, enter through the threshold, open Mnemos, inspect what is carried, deprecate what no longer fits, and return to the thread.

Mnemos.chat exists for one mission: continuity with consent. Memory that can grow, soften, correct itself, and keep the person or resident whole across time.
